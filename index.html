How Distill actually works

Distill uses extractive summarization: every sentence in your text is scored, and the top-scoring ones are kept in their original order. Nothing is rewritten or generated, so you're always reading your own words.

Each sentence's score combines:

  • Keyword weight — how many important, frequently-repeated words it contains.
  • Position — opening and closing sentences get a small boost, since they usually carry framing or a conclusion.
  • Signal phrases — dates, numbers, and action language ("please," "by Friday," "need," "deadline") are weighted higher, since these usually carry the actual ask.
  • Length balance — very short filler sentences and overly long run-ons are both discounted relative to sentences of typical length.

This works best on text where the point is stated somewhere in plain prose — it won't catch an ask buried entirely in a signature block or an image. And it's still not the same as a human reading the text closely: for anything high-stakes, use it as a first pass, not a final judgment.

Everything above runs in JavaScript in your browser. Your text is never sent anywhere.
